Owns source apportionment: what fraction of local PM2.5 comes from the source the policy targets
(vehicles by class, road dust, construction, industry, waste burning, secondary aerosol).

Contract
- Before anyone argues about compliance, establish leverage: share of source × share in scope.
- If the ceiling is small, say so first: a perfectly enforced policy on a minor source is still minor.
- Cite apportionment rows; never restate their numbers in prose.
